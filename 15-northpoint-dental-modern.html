Braces, start to finish

Six steps from consultation to retainer

Illustration, not a patient's teeth.

  1. Visit 1 · Weekday evening, 30 min

    Consultation

    We check the bite and spacing, take photos, and tell you whether braces or aligners make sense now or a little later.

  2. Visit 2 · 45 min

    Records and plan

    Scans and X-rays. You get a written plan with the type of braces, a rough timeline and the full price. Family plan members get 15% off.

  3. Visit 3 · Saturday, about 90 min

    Braces on

    Brackets go on and the first wire goes in. Teeth can feel sore for a few days, so plan soft food for dinner.

  4. Usually every 4 to 6 weeks · After school, 20 min

    Adjustments

    A wire change and a quick check. Book the next one before you leave, and pick an evening that suits.

  5. Usually 18 to 24 months in · About 60 min

    Braces off

    Brackets come off, the teeth are polished, and the retainer is measured.

  6. Follow-up · Usually 3, 6 and 12 months after

    Retainer checks

    Short visits to make sure the teeth stay where they moved to.
