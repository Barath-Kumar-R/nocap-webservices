Care for every age in the household.

We added one service at a time, as the families we see needed it. The year beside each line is when it started.

  1. 2009

    General dentistry

    Check-ups, cleanings and fillings for the whole family.

  2. 2011

    Root canals and crowns

    Root canal treatment over a few visits, with crowns from a lab we have used for over a decade.

  3. 2014

    Dentures and bridges

    Full and partial dentures fitted over several visits, with adjustments included in the price.

  4. 2017

    Children’s dentistry

    A gentler first visit. Some of the children we see now belong to our first patients.

  5. 2019

    Braces and aligners

    Traditional braces and clear aligners, with the payment split across the treatment.

  6. 2022

    Implants

    Single-tooth implants, placed with a visiting oral surgeon who usually comes in once a month.
