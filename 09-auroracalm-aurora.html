Our approach

Care, paced to you.

Three habits we keep at every visit, whatever the treatment.

  1. Sedation, only if you want it

    Nitrous oxide or oral sedation, explained before the visit and never assumed. Your dentist checks first that it suits you.

  2. An hour, at least

    Each appointment is booked for at least 60 minutes, even a check-up.

  3. Nothing without a word first

    Each step is explained before it happens. Raise a hand at any point and we stop.
