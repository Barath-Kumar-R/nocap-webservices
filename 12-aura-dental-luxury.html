The first consult

A consult first. Treatment later, if you want it.

  1. Talk

    What bothers you about your smile, and what you would rather leave alone.

  2. Look

    Your teeth are looked at with the rest of your face: lip line, gum line, how much shows when you speak.

  3. Scan

    A 3D scan of your teeth, which you go through with the dentist on screen.

  4. Plan

    The cost of each stage, written down. Nobody expects you to book treatment on the day.

Before you book

Questions worth asking first.

Will veneers look like veneers?

They should not. The aim is that nobody can tell which of your teeth have them.

How white is too white?

Anything that stops looking like your teeth in daylight. If a shade would look painted on next to your skin, the dentist will say so.

Are aligner reviews done over video?

No. You come into the studio once a month, so the dentist checks your teeth and not a photo of them.

Can I come on a Sunday?

Yes, from 10 AM to 2 PM. The studio is closed on Mondays.
