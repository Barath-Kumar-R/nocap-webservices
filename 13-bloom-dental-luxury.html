Treatments

Three reasons people come in.

Every plan starts with a talk about how you want to feel afterwards: comfortable, confident, or simply not thinking about your teeth.

To keep things healthy

Gentle cleanings
Ultrasonic cleaning, with numbing gel offered every time.
Fillings and root canals
Tooth-coloured composite fillings matched to the teeth around them, and root canal treatment when a tooth needs it.

To change how your smile looks

Smile design
A consult that looks at your whole face before any veneer or bonding work is planned.
Whitening
In-chair whitening. The shade guide comes out first, so you agree on where to stop.
Clear aligners
Progress photos every month, with reviews here in the clinic.

If the dentist makes you nervous

Anxiety-aware care
For people who have stayed away for years. You can ask for a break at any point, and sedation options are talked through before anything is decided.

What to expect

Your first visit, start to finish.

  1. One

    Arrive and have some tea

    Fifteen minutes to settle in. The forms come to you beforehand, so there is nothing to fill in at the desk.

  2. Two

    Talk, then a scan

    You say what has been bothering you. The scan comes after, and the dentist explains what it shows.

  3. Three

    Treatment, at your pace

    Breaks are offered along the way. Nothing is hurried to make room for the next appointment.

  4. Four

    A note to take home

    A written care plan, and a WhatsApp number for questions afterwards.
